AI Background Changer replaces the surroundings of a portrait, product, or other foreground subject with a setting you describe.

[Open AI Background Changer](https://magichour.ai/products/ai-background-changer)

## How to use it

1. Upload the image whose background you want to change.
2. Describe the new location, colors, environment, or mood.
3. Generate options, inspect the subject and background, and download your preferred result.

## Example prompt

```text
Replace the background with a softly blurred office. Keep the person, clothing, and pose unchanged, with lighting that matches the face.
```

## Check the result

Describe the setting and light. Check hair edges, transparent materials, reflections, and contact shadows.

## API availability

There is no dedicated Background Changer endpoint in the [public API specification](https://magichour.ai/docs/api/openapi.json). Use [AI Image Editor](/api-reference/image-projects/ai-image-editor) for prompt-based background changes. [Background Remover](/api-reference/image-projects/image-background-remover) handles background removal.
