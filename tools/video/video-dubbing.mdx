## Overview

[AI Video Dubbing](https://magichour.ai/products/ai-video-dubbing) replaces a video's original speech with translated audio modeled on the speaker's voice. The website lists 53 languages and includes lip synchronization in the dubbing workflow.

## How it works

1. Upload a video with intelligible spoken dialogue.
2. Select the language for the new voice track.
3. Generate the dub. The tool transcribes, translates, recreates the voice, and adjusts mouth movements.
4. Listen to the new track and check mouth synchronization before downloading.

Have a fluent speaker check the translation, especially names and numbers. Check mouth alignment during close-ups and fast speech.

## Dubbing and translation

The website combines translation with voice replacement and lip sync. [Video Translator](/tools/video/video-translator) focuses on language selection; Video Dubbing focuses on the replacement spoken performance.

## API availability

The [published API specification](https://magichour.ai/docs/api/openapi.json) has no dedicated Video Dubbing endpoint.

For an existing replacement audio track, the [Lip Sync API](/api-reference/video-projects/lip-sync) offers mouth synchronization. Translation and replacement voice generation are not part of that endpoint.
