## Overview

[AI Video Dubbing](https://magichour.ai/products/ai-video-dubbing) replaces a video's original speech with translated audio modeled on the speaker's voice. The website lists 53 languages and includes lip synchronization in the dubbing workflow.

## How It Works

1. **Upload your video** - Choose footage with intelligible spoken dialogue.
2. **Choose a language** - Select the language for the new voice track.
3. **Generate the dub** - AI transcribes, translates, recreates the voice, and adjusts mouth movements.
4. **Review and download** - Listen to the new track and check the synchronized picture.

## Use Cases

- **Training videos** - Produce spoken versions for different language groups.
- **Ad localization** - Reuse a performance with dialogue for another market.
- **Creator content** - Adapt interviews, presentations, or narrated videos.

## Best Practices

<Tip>Review the spoken result with someone fluent in the target language before publishing.</Tip>

- Start with audio where the main speaker is easy to understand.
- Listen for missed names, changed numbers, and awkward sentence timing.
- Check mouth alignment during close-ups and fast speech.
- Compare the complete output with the source, including pauses and scene transitions.

## Dubbing and Translation

The website combines translation with voice replacement and lip sync. [Video Translator](/tools/video/video-translator) focuses on language selection; Video Dubbing focuses on the replacement spoken performance.

## API Availability

The [published API specification](https://magichour.ai/docs/api/openapi.json) has no dedicated Video Dubbing endpoint.

For an existing replacement audio track, the [Lip Sync API](/api-reference/video-projects/lip-sync) offers mouth synchronization. Translation and replacement voice generation are not part of that endpoint.
