## Overview

[Music Video Generator](https://magichour.ai/products/ai-music-video-generator) turns an uploaded song into a music video with scenes informed by lyrics and editing aligned to the beat. You can guide the visual style with a concept and reference images, and enable lip sync for selected performance scenes.

## How It Works

1. **Upload a song** - Provide the audio track for the video.
2. **Set a creative direction** - Describe the concept or choose a visual style.
3. **Add references** - Supply images for characters, locations, products, or the desired look.
4. **Choose lip sync** - Enable it for singing scenes, or leave it off for other visual treatments.
5. **Generate and review** - The tool creates and assembles scenes around the track.

## Use Cases

- **Release promotion** - Create a video or social clip for a song.
- **Visual storytelling** - Explore imagery inspired by lyrical themes.
- **Concept development** - Compare visual directions for the same music.

## Best Practices

<Tip>
  Write a coherent visual concept before generating: subject, setting, mood, and a small set of
  recurring motifs.
</Tip>

- Use references that agree on the appearance of recurring subjects.
- Choose whether the result should emphasize performance, narrative, or abstract imagery.
- Review scene-to-scene identity and continuity, as well as individual frames.
- Watch the complete result with sound to judge pacing and performance timing.

## API Availability

The [published API specification](https://magichour.ai/docs/api/openapi.json) has no dedicated Music Video Generator endpoint.

The [Audio to Video API](/api-reference/video-projects/audio-to-video) is a related audio-driven generation tool. Its documented request does not expose this website workflow's scene assembly, lyric-aware editing, or optional performance-scene controls.
