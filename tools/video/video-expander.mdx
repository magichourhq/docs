## Overview

[AI Video Expander](https://magichour.ai/products/ai-video-expander) generates new surroundings beyond a video's edges to fit a different aspect ratio. Use it to adapt footage for vertical, widescreen, square, or portrait layouts while keeping the original subject visible.

## How It Works

1. **Upload a clip** - Choose the video you want to reframe.
2. **Choose an aspect ratio** - Select the layout needed for your destination.
3. **Generate the expansion** - AI fills the surrounding area with new visual content.
4. **Review and download** - Inspect the new edges throughout the clip.

## Use Cases

- **Social formats** - Adapt footage for vertical posts or widescreen playback.
- **Ad layouts** - Make room around a subject for copy or product details.
- **Tight framing** - Add surrounding context to an otherwise cramped composition.

## Best Practices

<Tip>
  Start with a shot that has a clear subject, consistent lighting, and steady camera movement.
</Tip>

- Split a sequence at scene cuts before evaluating individual shots.
- Check frame borders during movement, especially when objects enter or leave the image.
- Keep essential text and logos away from newly generated regions until you review them.
- Treat new surroundings as generated imagery, rather than recovered original footage.

## Expanding Versus Extending

Video Expander changes frame dimensions. [Video Extender](/tools/video/video-extender) adds time by generating footage after the source clip.

## API Availability

The [published API specification](https://magichour.ai/docs/api/openapi.json) has no dedicated Video Expander endpoint.

For supported edits within a clip, see the [AI Video Editor API](/api-reference/video-projects/ai-video-editor). Its documented interface does not expose video expansion.
