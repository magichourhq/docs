## Overview

[AI Voice Changer](https://magichour.ai/products/ai-voice-changer) transforms an existing recording into a selected voice.

## How it works

1. Upload an audio or video file, or record audio in the browser.
2. Choose a replacement voice from the available library.
3. Generate, preview, and download the transformed audio.

The upload control lists supported formats, including MP3, WAV, M4A, and MP4.

## Choose a voice tool

| Input                           | Tool                                            |
| :------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------------- |
| Existing speech to transform    | Voice Changer                                   |
| Text to speak in a preset voice | [Voice Generator](/tools/audio/voice-generator) |
| Voice sample and new text       | [Voice Cloner](/tools/audio/voice-cloner)       |

## Availability and pricing

The [web tool](https://magichour.ai/products/ai-voice-changer) offers a free browser trial. Check it for current generation limits, available voices, and plan options.

## API availability

Voice Changer is available in the web app. The published [API reference](/api-reference/overview) does not include a voice-changing endpoint. Voice Generator and Voice Cloner have separate APIs for generating speech from text.
