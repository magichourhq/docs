## Overview

[AI Music Generator](https://magichour.ai/products/ai-music-generator) creates music from a description. Generate a vocal song with your lyrics, or choose Instrumental for music without singing.

## How it works

1. Describe the genre, mood, instruments, and pace you want.
2. Add lyrics for a vocal track, or enable Instrumental.
3. Generate, listen to the preview, and download your track.

## Prompt tips

Use specific musical direction, for example:

> Relaxed instrumental with soft piano, brushed drums, and a slow tempo for a podcast introduction.

For narration, request sparse instrumentation so the music leaves room for speech.

## Availability and pricing

The [product page](https://magichour.ai/products/ai-music-generator) offers a free browser trial and lists current generation limits. Check the selected plan for track duration, credits, and commercial-use terms.

## API availability

Music Generator is available in the web app. The published [API reference](/api-reference/overview) does not include a music-generation endpoint.
