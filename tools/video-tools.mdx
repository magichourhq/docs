## Which motion tool should I use?

- **Make a photo move:** [Image to Video](/tools/video/image-to-video).
- **Make a portrait speak:** [Talking Photo](/tools/video/talking-photo), with a portrait and speech audio.
- **Change speech in footage:** [Lip Sync](/tools/video/lip-sync), with a video and replacement audio.
- **Create stylized, morphing visuals:** [Animation](/tools/video/animation).
