## Compare billing models

Magic Hour offers prepaid subscriptions and usage-based billing.

| Feature           | Subscription                          | Usage-based                                |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------ | :----------------------------------------- |
| Payment timing    | Pay before using credits              | Pay after using credits                    |
| Credits           | Fixed allotment                       | Charged as consumed                        |
| Credit expiration | Unused credits do not expire          | No prepaid credit allotment                |
| Price             | Fixed plan price                      | Banded rates, up to 50% below base         |
| Billing frequency | Monthly or yearly                     | Monthly, or earlier at a billing threshold |
| Setup             | Complete checkout on the pricing page | Contact Magic Hour for assisted setup      |

## Subscription pricing

A monthly payment adds one monthly credit allotment. An annual payment adds twelve monthly
allotments upfront. Unused credits remain in your account after cancellation and do not expire.

| Plan     | Monthly | Annual    | Monthly credit allotment |
| :------- | ------: | :-------- | -----------------------: |
| Creator  |     $19 | $12/month |                   12,000 |
| Pro      |     $39 | $25/month |                   25,000 |
| Business |     $99 | $66/month |                   70,000 |

Annual Creator costs $144, Pro costs $300, and Business costs $792. The annual payment adds 144,000
Creator credits, 300,000 Pro credits, or 840,000 Business credits upfront.

Paid plans remove watermarks, include commercial use, and unlock higher-resolution options on each
tool. See [subscription pricing](/billing/subscription-pricing) for upload limits, concurrent job
limits, credit packs, and checkout instructions.

## Usage-based pricing

Usage-based billing charges for credits consumed. Rates use bands, and the highest band is 50% below
the base rate. Invoices normally issue monthly. High-use accounts may receive an earlier invoice
after reaching a billing threshold.

Usage-based billing requires assisted setup. Email
[support@magichour.ai](mailto:support@magichour.ai) or use the Intercom chat widget on the Magic Hour
website. See [usage-based pricing](/billing/usage-based-pricing) for per-credit rates and setup.

## Choose a model

1. Estimate how many credits your requests will consume. Use [models and credit costs](/api-reference/models) for each model and resolution.
2. Check which plan unlocks the resolution, upload size, and concurrent job limit your workload needs. See [resolution limits](/billing/resolution-limits).
3. Compare the subscription price with the [API cost calculator](https://magichour.ai/api?ref=docs-billing-overview#api-cost-calculator). Include credit packs if your expected use exceeds a subscription allotment.
