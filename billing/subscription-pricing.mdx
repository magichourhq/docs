## How Subscription Pricing Works

**Simple concept:** Pay upfront for a set amount of credits that you can use throughout your subscription period. Perfect for predictable usage patterns.

### **The Basics**

1. **Choose your plan** based on monthly credit volume and which resolution options you need
2. **Select billing frequency** - monthly or yearly (annual billing is discounted)
3. **Pay upfront** and receive all credits immediately
4. Use purchased credits anytime. The pricing page lists them as non-expiring. Check separate terms for promotional credits.
5. **Auto-renewal** ensures continuous access (can be cancelled anytime)

### **Key Benefits**

✅ **Predictable costs** - Know exactly what you'll pay each month/year
✅ **Credits upfront** - Get all your credits immediately to use anytime
✅ **Yearly savings** - Annual billing is discounted
✅ **No usage tracking** - Use credits at your own pace
✅ **Higher resolution options** - Paid plans unlock higher-resolution choices on each tool

### **How Payment Methods Differ**

| Subscription plans                  | Credit packs                      | Usage-based billing              |
| :---------------------------------- | :-------------------------------- | :------------------------------- |
| Recurring monthly or annual payment | One-time purchase                 | Monthly invoice after use        |
| Credits and plan benefits included  | Credits without a subscription    | Charges for credits consumed     |
| Prepaid credits never expire        | Prepaid credits never expire      | No prepaid balance               |
| Best for predictable, ongoing usage | Best for one-time work or top-ups | Best for variable or high volume |

## Need More Credits?

If you use more credits than your plan includes, you can purchase additional credit packs anytime.

### **Credit Packs**

| Pack     | Credits | Price | Credits per dollar |
| :------- | ------: | ----: | -----------------: |
| Starter  |   4,000 |   $10 |                400 |
| Standard |  12,000 |   $30 |                400 |
| Large    |  32,000 |   $80 |                400 |
| Max      |  64,000 |  $160 |                400 |

These are the current USD list prices on the [pricing page](https://magichour.ai/pricing#credit-pack).
Review any account-specific offer at checkout. Purchased credits go into your account, and the
pricing page lists them as non-expiring. Packs do not include subscription benefits other than removing watermarks.
A pack alone does not unlock models, resolutions, or other features reserved for a
paid plan.

### **When to Buy Credit Packs**

- **Seasonal spikes** in usage
- **One-time projects** requiring extra credits
- **Testing higher usage** before upgrading plans
- **End-of-month** credit shortfall

<Note>
  **Tip:** If you consistently need credit packs, consider upgrading to the next plan tier for
  better value.
</Note>

## Choosing a Plan

Pick the plan whose monthly credits cover your expected volume, then check that it unlocks the
resolution options you need. Higher resolution on the same plan costs more credits, so a 1080p
workload burns through an allotment faster than 480p. If you regularly exhaust your allowance and
buy credit packs, the next tier up is usually cheaper than the packs.

For API workloads with variable volume, compare against
[usage-based billing](/billing/usage-based-pricing), which invoices usage monthly and applies
graduated volume discounts. It currently requires setup assistance.

## Getting Started

Ready to subscribe? Here's how to set up your subscription in minutes.

<Steps>
<Step title="Visit the Pricing Page">
  Go to [magichour.ai/pricing](https://magichour.ai/pricing?ref=docs-subscription-pricing) to see all available plans and current pricing.

![Example pricing page](./images/pricing-page-1.jpg)

</Step>

<Step title="Choose Billing Frequency">
  **Monthly**: Pay monthly, cancel anytime. **Yearly**: Pay upfront at the discount shown on the
  live pricing page.

![Billing-frequency control](./images/pricing-page-2.jpg)

</Step>

<Step title="Select Your Plan">
  Choose based on monthly credits and which resolution options you need:
  - **Creator**: $19/month ($12/month billed annually), 12,000 credits
  - **Pro**: $39/month ($25/month billed annually), 25,000 credits
  - **Business**: $99/month ($66/month billed annually), 70,000 credits

![Plan selection buttons](./images/pricing-page-3.jpg)

Click the button for your chosen plan to proceed to checkout.

</Step>

<Step title="Complete Payment">
  Choose a payment method available at checkout, such as a credit or debit card, Apple Pay, or
  Google Pay. Availability depends on your location. Review the billing interval, total, and
  included credits, then click **Subscribe**.
</Step>

<Step title="Access Your Credits">
  After successful payment:
  - Open [My Plan](https://magichour.ai/my-plan) to confirm your plan and credit balance
  - Start using the API once your payment and credits appear
  - If your plan or credits do not appear after payment, refresh, sign in with the payment email, and contact support

![Payment Successful](./images/subscription-successful.png)

</Step>

</Steps>

## Managing Your Subscription

### **Tracking Credit Usage**

Monitor your remaining credits in multiple ways:

1. Visit [My Plan](https://magichour.ai/my-plan) to see your credit allocation, billing interval, and renewal details
2. **Any signed-in page**: Check credit balance in the top navigation

![Create Page](./images/subscription-credits.png)

### **Subscription Management**

Open [My Plan](https://magichour.ai/my-plan), select an available plan and billing interval, and
review the effective date, total, included credits, and any adjustment before confirming. Available
changes depend on your current subscription.

To stop renewal, select **Cancel subscription** and complete the confirmation flow. Active
subscriptions normally retain paid benefits until the end of the billing period shown in the
confirmation. Cancellation does not automatically refund an existing payment.

See the [plan-change guide](https://help.magichour.ai/en/articles/12612594-how-to-upgrade-or-downgrade-your-plan)
and [cancellation guide](https://help.magichour.ai/en/articles/12612584-how-to-cancel-your-subscription)
for current steps.

### **What Happens When You Downgrade or Cancel**

When your subscription ends or you downgrade to the free Basic plan:

**✅ You Keep:**

- Cancellation does not remove your remaining account credits. Promotional terms can differ.
- **API access** - continue making requests with remaining credits
- You keep account access. Download any outputs you need to keep, since generated files have separate retention limits.

**❌ You Lose (at end of billing cycle):**

- **Higher resolution options** - revert to the Free option on each tool
- Free video/audio outputs may include watermarks. A separate credit pack can still provide watermark removal.
- **Monthly credit allocation** - starter credits and rewards vary by account; check your current balance
- Future generations follow the free plan terms. Priority support and other paid-plan benefits end.

You can upgrade again through My Plan. Review the benefits and billing terms shown for the new plan.

### **Important Notes**

<Warning>
  **Insufficient Credits**: API calls will be rejected if your account doesn't have enough credits
  for the requested operation.
</Warning>

<Info>
  The pricing page lists purchased credits as non-expiring. Bonus and promotional
  credits can have separate terms. See [Understanding
  Credits](https://help.magichour.ai/en/articles/12612555-understanding-credits) and the terms in My
  Plan or checkout for your balance. Remaining credits do not keep paid-plan features unlocked after
  cancellation.
</Info>

<Tip>
  Check your balance in My Plan and usage analytics in the Developer Hub before starting a large
  batch of API requests.
</Tip>
